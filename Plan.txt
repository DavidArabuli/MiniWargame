Player - as a side. Can see board, armies, cities. Can make actions.
Player plays as an army froim the start.
Player has cities, armies and resources (just gold for now). 
Player can move or attack AI armies and cities.

Enemy - AI - has cities only for now. Later will have armies. 
Will be passive for now. Later will be able to field armies and attack.

Cities - have location, name, strength, and economy - they produce gold each turn.

Cities will have an ownership flag. For now it will be only player owned vs AI (enemy) owned.

Player can hire armies in cities. 
Player`s army has strength, location, and can move or attack.
Player`s army strength is increased when he hires armies in cities.

--
On the app level:
City is a class with properties for location, name, strength, economy, and ownership. The player will have a list of cities they own, and the AI will have its own list of cities.
Player is a class, with properties for their army and resources (gold). 
The player can perform actions (class methods) such as moving armies, attacking enemy cities, and hiring new armies in their own cities.
